Visitor analytics

Arivora uses first-party analytics to estimate visits and distinct browsers. Opening or refreshing the app records one view. A random browser identifier is stored locally for up to 90 days; the server stores a hash of that identifier, the visit time and a broad traffic-source category. Analytics records are retained for up to 90 days and are accessible only to our administrators.

These analytics records do not include your name, email, account ID, questions, exact referring URL or IP address. Normal hosting and security logs are separate. We do not use this tracking for advertising.

We respect your browser's Do Not Track and Global Privacy Control settings. When either is enabled, no analytics event is sent. Blocked browser storage also disables tracking. Clearing browser storage removes the local identifier but may cause a later visit to count as a new visitor.
